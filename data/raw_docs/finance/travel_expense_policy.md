# Corporate Travel, Meals and Expense Reimbursement Policy

## 1. Flight and Transportation Standards
All business travel must be booked via Navan (formerly TripActions) at least 14 days in advance to secure favorable enterprise rates.
- **Domestic Flights (<6 hours flight duration):** Economy class is mandatory for all personnel.
- **International / Long-Haul Flights (>6 continuous hours):** Premium Economy is permitted with Director approval. Business class is reserved exclusively for flights exceeding 10 hours with scheduled client meetings within 12 hours of landing.
- **Rideshare and Rental Cars:** Uber/Lyft standard ride types are reimbursable. Rental car reservations are restricted to intermediate/sedan category unless traveling with 3+ colleagues.

## 2. Daily Meals & Incidentals Per Diem (M&IE)
Daily meal allowances operate under a fixed per diem cap:
- **Domestic Travel:** Up to $75 USD per full travel day ($15 Breakfast, $25 Lunch, $35 Dinner). Alcohol is non-reimbursable unless hosting approved client entertainment.
- **International Travel:** Up to $120 USD per travel day.
- **Receipt Submission Deadline:** All expense reports, accompanied by itemized tax receipts, must be submitted via Concur within 14 calendar days of trip conclusion. Submissions past 30 days are subject to automatic forfeiture.
