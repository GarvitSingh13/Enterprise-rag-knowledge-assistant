# STRICTLY CONFIDENTIAL: Executive Compensation & Severance Schedules

## 1. Scope and Access Restrictions
This document contains sensitive material regarding executive leadership equity cliffs, severance multiplier formulas, and board-level change-of-control provisions.
Access is restricted strictly to C-Suite Officers (CEO, CFO, CTO) and Members of the Board of Directors.

## 2. Annual Performance Incentive Plan (APIP)
Executive cash bonuses are determined according to annual corporate EBITDA milestones:
- Threshold attainment (80% of annual recurring revenue target): 50% target payout.
- Target attainment (100% ARR target): 100% target payout.
- Maximum outperformance (125%+ ARR target): 200% target payout cap.

## 3. Equity Acceleration and Double-Trigger Provisions
In the event of a change-of-control transaction (Merger, Acquisition, or Sale of Assets):
- 100% of unvested Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) accelerate immediately upon termination without cause within 12 months following transaction close (Double-Trigger Clause).
- Severance payout equals 18 months of base salary plus prorated target bonus, along with 18 months of subsidized COBRA healthcare continuation.
