# Corporate Health, Dental and Wellness Benefits Overview

## 1. Medical and Dental Coverage
Our comprehensive health insurance plan provides coverage for employees and eligible dependents:
- **In-Network Deductible:** $500 individual / $1,000 family.
- **Dental Benefits:** 100% coverage for routine preventive cleanings (2 per year). 80% coverage for minor restorative procedures up to an annual maximum of $2,000 USD per covered individual. Orthodontia is covered at 50% with a lifetime maximum of $3,000 USD.
- **Optical & Vision Care:** Vision examination copay is $10. Eyeglass frames and contact lenses receive a $350 USD biennial allowance (every 24 months).

## 2. Mental Health & Employee Assistance Program (EAP)
We partner with ModernHealth to provide confidential mental health support:
- 6 free 1-on-1 therapy sessions per calendar year for every employee and their immediate family members.
- Unlimited access to guided meditation, stress management courses, and licensed certified life coaches.
- 24/7 crisis hotline accessible toll-free at 1-800-555-WELL.
