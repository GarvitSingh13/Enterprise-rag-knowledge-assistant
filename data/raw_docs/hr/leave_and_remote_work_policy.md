# Global Employee Leave and Remote Working Policy

## 1. Annual Leave Entitlement
Full-time regular employees accrue 24 paid annual leave days per calendar year. 
- A maximum of 5 unused leave days may be carried over into the following year, expiring by March 31st.
- Leave requests exceeding 5 consecutive business days require manager approval at least 14 days in advance via Workday.
- Sick leave: Employees receive up to 10 paid sick leave days annually with no medical certificate required for absences under 2 days.

## 2. Remote and Hybrid Work Guidelines
Employees working in hybrid-eligible roles may work remotely up to 3 days per week.
- **Core Collaboration Hours:** All employees, regardless of location, must remain accessible on Slack and email between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM Eastern Standard Time (EST).
- **Home Office Setup Allowance:** Full-time permanent employees are eligible for a one-time reimbursement of up to $500 USD for ergonomic equipment (monitors, chairs, standing desk converters). Receipts must be submitted within 60 days of hire.
- **Work From Anywhere:** Employees may work from an approved international location for up to 30 calendar days per rolling 12 months, subject to tax compliance clearance.
